Questions

Straight answers.

How is 3DOT different from a regular marketing agency?

Most agencies sell one channel, like ads or social, and measure themselves on activity. We work on the whole path from first click to closed sale, including the systems behind it, and we measure ourselves on revenue. We also build and run the CRM ourselves, so nothing gets lost between "the marketing people" and "the tech people."

Do I have to switch to your CRM?

No. If you're on HubSpot or another platform that works, we'll build there. Our own platform is an option for businesses that want one place for contacts, email, texting, pages and automations without a stack of subscriptions.

How fast will I see results?

The quickest wins usually come from follow-up speed and re-engaging your existing list, and those can go live in the first few weeks. Bigger shifts, like a better-converting site or a steady nurture program, build over the first quarter. We'll tell you up front which is which.

What does it cost?

Every engagement is scoped and priced before we start, so you know the number up front. The Diagnostic is a fixed fee. Builds are priced as a project. Run is a monthly retainer. We'll give you real numbers on the first call.

Will I own my data and accounts?

Yes. Your contacts, content, domains and ad accounts belong to you. If we ever part ways, you get a full export and every login handed back.

Is texting customers legal?

It is when it's done right. We set up carrier registration (A2P 10DLC), collect clear consent on every form, honor STOP replies automatically and keep records. Every marketing email includes your mailing address and an unsubscribe link.
